Optional analytics: your choice

Facet helps us understand which public pages people read and how they find this site. It stays off unless you allow it. Declining does not change your access.

If allowed, Facet records page views, their time, the configured public page path, limited source categories and approved referring domains. It does not collect country, browser, operating system or device categories in this trial.

Facet event records do not contain raw IP addresses, raw browser identification strings, full referring URLs, URL queries, precise location, visitor IDs or linked activity across websites. A random key identifies each event to prevent duplicate delivery, not to recognize you. Blockers and network failures can prevent recording.

Active events are scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Cloudflare D1 recovery can retain deleted records for up to seven additional days. Outages may delay deletion; collection pauses when retention checks become stale. Cloudflare processes network information to deliver this service.

Your choice is saved separately for this app in this browser. Withdraw permission to stop future collection; previously sent events remain until scheduled deletion. Browser privacy signals override permission. A new notice version requires a new choice. This controls Facet on Living Space only.
