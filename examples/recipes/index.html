Can I change the layout?

Yes. Original content stays in HTML.

Selection changes spatial presentation, not modal DOM focus. Explicit close restores its invoking control; ordinary field updates do not move focus. Compact and reduced views use document layout. Drag only labelled grips; keyboard alternatives remain available.
